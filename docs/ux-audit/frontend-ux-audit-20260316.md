# Frontend UI/UX Audit — 16 Mar 2026

This document captures findings from a self-driven audit of the DocTrack SaaS frontend, benchmarked against PandaDoc-style document workflow experiences and cross-checked against the platform's business logic.

## Scope & Method
- **Flows covered:** registration/onboarding, document upload & forwarding, workflow review actions (approve/comment/forward), dashboards & analytics, profile/security settings, shared navigation/toast patterns.
- **Benchmarks:** PandaDoc and similar workflow SaaS products for progressive disclosure, delegation clarity, and analytics storytelling.
- **Verification:** Compared Blade templates with their corresponding controllers/validators to confirm that UI constraints line up with enforced business rules.

## Benchmark & UX Observations
1. **Workflow builder vs PandaDoc:** PandaDoc provides a summary sidebar (recipients, order, actions) before sending. DocTrack's forwarding view lacks an overview or confirmation modal; once a user scrolls through batches, it is hard to verify recipients. Adding a sticky “Review & Send” drawer with aggregated recipients would reduce errors.
2. **Delegation comprehension:** PandaDoc visually distinguishes advisory vs approval steps. DocTrack currently uses small radio cards without explaining consequences (especially since they are non-functional). Introduce tooltips/examples and make delegation depth warnings more prominent (badge near the submit CTA).
3. **Analytics storytelling:** Company dashboard cards already show trend deltas, but charts lack labeled thresholds or annotations. Consider sparkline tooltips and empty-state storytelling (e.g., "No office activity yet — invite teammates") to match PandaDoc’s polished reporting.
4. **Form focus management:** Multi-step registration uses custom validation but never re-focuses on the first invalid field; PandaDoc-grade flows usually focus and announce errors. Update the JS helpers to move focus and leverage `aria-live` regions for assistive tech.

## Suggested Next Steps
1. **Fix business-logic mismatches (Issues #1-3) before adding new UX polish** so forwarding and onboarding workflows behave predictably.
2. **Introduce a recipient summary & confirmation layer** for document forwarding to boost confidence in complex sequential chains.
3. **Iterate on analytics UX** with contextual empty states, tooltip benchmarks, and streamlined export affordances.
4. **Add accessibility & validation improvements** across multi-step forms (focus management, error summaries, keyboard-visible states).

These changes will align the frontend with backend rules, reduce user confusion during delegation, and bring the product closer to PandaDoc’s best-in-class workflow ergonomics.
